My Career Goals


Where Do I See Myself in 10 Years?

To look forward 10 years is very interesting to me considering I'm close to 30 years old already. In some cases I feel behind the curve yet I had a whole career in music prior to starting my bachelor's. That being said I have high expectations for what comes next, through every bit of my career as an individual in the workforce I find some key traits and titles I hope to have achieved. To start I've set the goal of a senior position in the field of software development to be my goal post, in what specific aspect? At this point, it could be one of two options. I love QA, always have and always will. With this considered, I think I'd be very happy as a senior test engineer, managing groups of other QA engineers to create powerful testing tools. I've always strived for higher degrees of efficiency and in today's world, automated testing is incredibly important to implement given the rise of AI. It's my guess that more focused and stringent QA will be required of software companies to ensure that the mistakes made by AI are corralled and dealt with accordingly. Regardless, a position managing a team to achieve this is very appealing to me long term, although not the only result that would make me happy.

In addition to QA, my experience thus far as a developer has been varied in which end I work on; both frontend and backend. Being a full stack developer is difficult and at times stressful. My experience so far in the industry has been defined by working alone, something I'd hope to leave in the past as in 10 years I'd be happy working with a team as a senior full stack developer. Now the question is less about the role and more about the product as this position has so many varied tasks and responsibilities.

As a full stack developer you get to do a little bit of everything, which in my case is never a bad thing. I really enjoy most aspects of development; frontend, backend and everything in between. The main thing that brings me joy in programming is building interesting tools and products, watching them turn from ideas to something tangible. This is always a varied process that holds so many new challenges within every project no matter how similar or dissimilar they are from one another. While I may work for a long period on one specific job, full stack developers can always know there will be something new and different on the horizon. This kind of flexibility and variety that is offered in this specialization will keep the work fresh and fun. As a long term goal for a career I can't think of anything more important than a consistently evolving role, one that keeps the brain sharp and excited to work on problems each day.

In short, I can say confidently that finding myself in either of these positions ten years down the line will be the mark of a full career. While in school, it's easy to daydream about where my life is headed but I always remind myself that won't happen unless I focus on the work that must be done today. I am extremely motivated to make my dreams a reality and will look back with pride once all is said and done.
