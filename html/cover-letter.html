Holden Prines Cover Letter

To whomever it may concern, My name is Holden Prine, and I am a full stack developer from Los Angeles, California. My journey into development began in the audio field, where I spent years crafting professional soundscapes for podcasts, film scores, and more. During that time, I wrote custom C/C++ scripts to build plugins and audio tools, sparking an interest in coding that I later pursued professionally. As the pandemic shifted the job market, I pivoted to web development, enrolling in LEARN Academy's bootcamp where I worked with React and Ruby On Rails, transitioning into developer roles at various startups. Currently, I am continuing this work whilst pursuing my B.S. in Computer Science from WGU, completing the program by the end of 2026. Throughout this time, I have built and maintained web and mobile applications using React, React Native, Next.js, Angular, and Python developing reusable components and scalable UI systems utilizing libraries like DailyUI, Framer, Bootstrap, Webpack and tsParticles. I have extensive experience implementing custom animations with TypeScript libraries, enhancing user experiences through smooth, interactive design elements. On the styling front, I have worked with CSS3, SCSS and Tailwind CSS, maintaining and refining design systems to ensure consistency and performance across applications. Beyond frontend development, my experience with backend technologies and architectures is wide spread. My last role had me engaging in the architecting process, identifying schema specifications and 3rd party API integrations necessary for the project. These were then built out specifically with Node, Express, and postgreSQL, which was used to manage data living on an AWS server. This experience, among others, have allowed me to develop a greater understanding of the cohesion needed to create and maintain scalable full stack applications. Most recently, I developed and maintained a platform focused on empowering women with self- defense skills. This project reinforced my passion for user-first design and collaboration. Working closely with PMs like Allie Burch, I learned to refine my development process balancing efficiency, maintainability, and creativity. Her emphasis on direct, thoughtful collaboration helped shape my approach to software engineering, pushing me to take on new challenges with confidence. Most importantly, I bring a user-centric approach to product development, considering their connection to what I'm building through every step of the process. This has contributed to my success as a developer, making lasting impacts on the start ups I've been a part of. This kind of contribution is something I want to continue doing and hope to have that opportunity in my next role. Thank you for your consideration and look forward to learning how I can best fit and apply my skills to your organization.
